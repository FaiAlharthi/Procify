# Example RFP: Customer Service Call Center Services

**Issuing Organization:** Summitview Utilities Cooperative
**RFP Reference:** SUC26-09

## 1. Objective
Summitview Utilities Cooperative seeks a qualified call center service provider to manage customer service inquiries, billing support, and outage reporting on behalf of the cooperative, serving approximately 45,000 residential and commercial accounts.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Inbound call handling for billing inquiries, service requests, and outage reporting
- Integration with the cooperative's existing customer information and billing system
- After-hours and emergency outage call handling
- Monthly call volume and customer satisfaction reporting

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Provider must guarantee maximum average call wait times during normal and peak periods
- Call center staff must complete cooperative-specific training prior to handling live calls
- Provider must maintain call recording for quality assurance, in compliance with applicable privacy regulations
- System integration must support real-time account lookup during calls

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-call or per-minute pricing structure, with any premium pricing for after-hours/emergency call handling itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service transition must be completed within 4 months of contract award, including a 3-week parallel-run period with the cooperative's current call handling process.
