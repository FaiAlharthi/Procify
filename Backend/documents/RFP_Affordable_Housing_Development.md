# Example RFP: Affordable Housing Development Partner

**Issuing Organization:** Brightwater Housing Authority
**RFP Reference:** BHA26-30

## 1. Objective
Brightwater Housing Authority seeks a qualified development partner to design, finance, and construct a 90-unit affordable housing development on Authority-owned land, supporting the region's affordable housing strategy targets.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Design and construction of a 90-unit residential development with a defined mix of unit sizes
- Site servicing and infrastructure development
- Coordination of applicable affordable housing financing programs
- Property management transition planning for post-construction operations

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Design must comply with applicable accessibility and affordable housing design standards
- Developer must demonstrate prior experience with at least two comparable affordable housing developments
- Construction must meet defined energy efficiency standards appropriate to regional building codes
- Developer must disclose financing structure and any public funding program participation

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a detailed development pro forma, including construction costs, financing structure, and any requested public contribution or land value consideration.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Design and permitting must be completed within 10 months of contract award, with construction completed within an additional 18 months.
