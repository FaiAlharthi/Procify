# Example RFP: Wildfire Risk Assessment and Mitigation Planning

**Issuing Organization:** Pinehollow Regional District
**RFP Reference:** PRD26-16

## 1. Objective
Pinehollow Regional District seeks a qualified environmental consulting firm to conduct a comprehensive wildfire risk assessment across district lands and adjacent residential areas, and to develop a multi-year wildfire mitigation and community preparedness plan.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Wildfire risk mapping across district lands, identifying high-risk zones
- Assessment of vegetation management and fuel reduction priorities
- Development of a community wildfire preparedness and evacuation planning framework
- Stakeholder engagement sessions with affected communities

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Firm must demonstrate prior experience conducting wildfire risk assessments for comparable regional districts
- Risk mapping must use recognized wildfire risk modeling methodology
- Mitigation plan must align with applicable provincial/state wildfire management guidelines
- Firm must provide GIS-based deliverables compatible with the district's existing mapping systems

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed fee for the full assessment and planning engagement, with stakeholder engagement session costs itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Risk assessment and mapping must be completed within 4 months of contract award, with the final mitigation plan delivered within 7 months, ahead of the following wildfire season.
