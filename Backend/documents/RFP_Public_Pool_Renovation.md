# Example RFP: Public Swimming Pool Renovation

**Issuing Organization:** City of Thornbury Recreation Department
**RFP Reference:** CTRD26-25

## 1. Objective
The City of Thornbury Recreation Department seeks a qualified contractor to renovate its 40-year-old public swimming pool facility, addressing aging mechanical systems, deck resurfacing, and accessibility improvements to extend the facility's operational life by at least 20 years.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Replacement of pool filtration and water treatment systems
- Resurfacing of pool deck and pool basin
- Installation of an accessible pool lift and ramped entry
- Renovation of locker room facilities, including accessibility upgrades

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All work must comply with current public pool health and safety regulations
- Filtration system must meet water quality standards for public aquatic facilities
- Accessibility features must comply with current applicable accessibility codes
- Contractor must propose a construction sequence minimizing the total closure period for the facility

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed-price bid broken down by major scope area (mechanical, deck/basin, accessibility, locker rooms), with a recommended contingency allowance.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Construction must be completed within a single off-season window (approximately 5 months) to allow the pool to reopen for the following public swimming season.
