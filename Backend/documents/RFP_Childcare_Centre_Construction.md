# Example RFP: New Childcare Centre Design and Construction

**Issuing Organization:** Districts of Hammond Early Learning Authority
**RFP Reference:** DHELA26-19

## 1. Objective
The Districts of Hammond Early Learning Authority seeks a qualified design-build contractor to construct a new 120-space childcare centre, supporting growing demand for licensed early learning spaces within the district.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Design and construction of a single-story childcare facility, including outdoor play areas
- Age-appropriate classroom design for infant, toddler, and preschool groupings
- Commercial kitchen facility for on-site meal preparation
- Fenced and secured outdoor play area meeting licensing requirements

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Facility design must comply with applicable childcare licensing and building code requirements
- Design must include required safety features (secure entry, sightlines, age-appropriate fixtures)
- Contractor must demonstrate prior experience with at least one comparable childcare facility project
- Materials and finishes must meet relevant indoor air quality and child safety standards

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed-price bid for the full design-build scope, with a detailed cost breakdown by major facility component.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Construction must be completed within 14 months of contract award, allowing time for licensing inspection and staff onboarding prior to opening.
