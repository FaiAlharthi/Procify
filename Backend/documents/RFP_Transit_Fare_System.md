# Example RFP: Public Transit Fare Collection System

**Issuing Organization:** Greater Millfield Transit Authority
**RFP Reference:** GMTA26-21

## 1. Objective
Greater Millfield Transit Authority seeks a vendor to supply and implement a modern contactless fare collection system across its bus and light rail network, replacing the current cash-only and paper transfer system to improve rider convenience and reduce fare evasion.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of contactless fare readers on all buses and rail station entry points
- Mobile app supporting fare payment and trip planning
- Backend fare reconciliation and reporting system
- Customer support for fare card issuance and account management

## 3. Technical Criteria
- System must support contactless bank card payment in addition to a dedicated transit fare card
- Platform must provide real-time ridership and revenue data to the Authority
- System must include fraud detection and fare evasion reporting capabilities
- Vendor must ensure rider payment data is handled in compliance with applicable financial data protection standards

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hardware and software implementation costs, separate from any ongoing per-transaction or licensing fees.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Implementation must be completed within 9 months of contract award, with a phased rollout beginning with the bus network followed by light rail integration.
