# Example RFP: Language Translation and Interpretation Services

**Issuing Organization:** Meadowbrook School Board
**RFP Reference:** MSB26-33

## 1. Objective
Meadowbrook School Board seeks a qualified language services provider to deliver translation and interpretation services supporting communication with multilingual families across its 22 schools, covering at minimum eight commonly spoken languages within the district.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Written translation of school communications, report cards, and enrollment documents
- On-demand telephone and video interpretation for parent-teacher meetings and administrative calls
- In-person interpretation for scheduled school events upon request
- Annual review of language demand to adjust supported languages as needed

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Interpreters and translators must hold relevant professional certification where applicable
- Provider must guarantee maximum response time for on-demand interpretation requests
- Provider must maintain confidentiality protocols appropriate for student and family information
- Provider must offer interpretation coverage during standard school hours and select evening events

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-page translation pricing and per-hour interpretation pricing, with any minimum booking fees clearly stated.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Services must be available for booking within 30 days of contract award, ahead of the start of the school year's parent-teacher conference period.
