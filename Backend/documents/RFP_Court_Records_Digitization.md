# Example RFP: Court Records Digitization Services

**Issuing Organization:** Granville County Court Administration
**RFP Reference:** GCCA26-29

## 1. Objective
Granville County Court Administration seeks a qualified vendor to digitize approximately 2.5 million pages of historical paper court records, creating a searchable digital archive while maintaining strict chain-of-custody and confidentiality requirements.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Secure transport and digitization of physical court records from the County archive facility
- Optical character recognition (OCR) processing to enable text searchability
- Indexing of records by case number, date, and document type
- Secure return or certified destruction of physical originals per County retention policy

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Vendor must maintain documented chain-of-custody procedures throughout the digitization process
- Digitization must meet a minimum defined resolution standard for legal document archival
- Vendor staff handling records must undergo background checks given the sensitive nature of court documents
- Digital records must be delivered in a format compatible with the County's existing records management system

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-page digitization pricing, with any additional costs for expedited processing or specialized document handling itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Digitization must be completed in phases by record category, with the full project completed within 14 months of contract award.
