# Example RFP: Airport Security Screening Equipment Upgrade

**Issuing Organization:** Crestline Regional Airport Authority
**RFP Reference:** CRAA26-17

## 1. Objective
Crestline Regional Airport Authority seeks a vendor to supply and install upgraded passenger and baggage security screening equipment across three screening checkpoints, improving throughput while maintaining compliance with current aviation security regulations.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of advanced imaging technology screening units at all three checkpoints
- Supply of upgraded baggage X-ray scanning equipment
- Integration with existing checkpoint queue management systems
- Staff training for screening personnel on new equipment operation

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Equipment must hold current applicable aviation security regulatory certification
- Vendor must provide minimum 99% equipment uptime guarantee given operational criticality
- Equipment must meet defined throughput targets (passengers screened per hour) per checkpoint
- Vendor must provide on-site technical support during initial 90 days of operation

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include equipment, installation, and training costs, along with a proposed maintenance contract covering preventive and emergency repair service.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation must be phased checkpoint-by-checkpoint to maintain continuous airport security operations, with full rollout completed within 5 months of contract award.
