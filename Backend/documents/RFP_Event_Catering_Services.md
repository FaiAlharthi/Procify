# Example RFP: Event Catering Services

**Issuing Organization:** State Capital Conference Centre
**RFP Reference:** SCCC26-11

## 1. Objective
The State Capital Conference Centre seeks a qualified catering company to provide on-site catering services for government and public events held at the centre, supporting an estimated 150 events annually ranging from small meetings to large receptions.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Full-service catering for events ranging from 10 to 500 guests
- Menu planning accommodating dietary restrictions and cultural/religious requirements
- Event staffing, including servers and bartenders where applicable
- Setup and breakdown of catering service areas

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All catering staff must hold current food safety certification
- Caterer must maintain documented allergen handling procedures
- Caterer must demonstrate prior experience with comparable government or institutional event catering
- Caterer must carry adequate liability insurance for on-site food service operations

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-person pricing tiers based on menu service level, with any additional staffing or equipment rental fees itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service agreement to begin within 60 days of contract award, with an initial trial event scheduled prior to full event calendar integration.
