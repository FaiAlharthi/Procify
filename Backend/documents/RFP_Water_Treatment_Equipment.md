# Example RFP: Water Treatment Equipment Upgrade

**Issuing Organization:** Bellmont Regional Water Authority
**RFP Reference:** BRWA26-03

## 1. Objective
Bellmont Regional Water Authority seeks a qualified vendor to supply and install upgraded filtration equipment at its primary treatment facility, increasing daily treatment capacity from 8 million to 12 million liters to meet projected population growth over the next decade.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of membrane filtration units rated for the required capacity increase
- Integration with existing SCADA control systems
- Decommissioning and removal of legacy filtration equipment
- Staff training on new equipment operation and maintenance procedures

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Equipment must meet current national drinking water treatment standards
- Vendor must provide minimum 5-year equipment warranty
- System must include redundant filtration capacity to allow maintenance without service interruption
- Vendor must disclose equipment failure rates from comparable installations

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized equipment, installation, and training costs, along with a proposed maintenance contract for years 1-5 post-installation.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation must be completed within 8 months of contract award, with a planned 2-week transition period during which both old and new systems remain operational in parallel.
