# Example RFP: Research Laboratory Equipment Supply

**Issuing Organization:** Brackenfield University, Faculty of Science
**RFP Reference:** BUFS26-05

## 1. Objective
Brackenfield University's Faculty of Science seeks a vendor to supply and install laboratory equipment for its newly constructed chemistry and biology research wing, supporting both undergraduate teaching labs and graduate research activities.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of fume hoods, lab benches, and safety equipment across 8 teaching and research labs
- Supply of specified analytical instrumentation for graduate research labs
- Calibration and commissioning of all installed equipment
- Staff and faculty training on new equipment operation

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All equipment must meet applicable laboratory safety and ventilation standards
- Vendor must provide calibration certificates for all analytical instrumentation
- Equipment must be compatible with the building's existing utility infrastructure (gas, electrical, ventilation)
- Vendor must provide minimum warranty terms appropriate to each equipment category

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized pricing by lab and equipment category, with installation and commissioning costs itemized separately from equipment costs.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Equipment supply and installation must be completed within 5 months of contract award, ahead of the start of the fall academic term.
