# Example RFP: Recreation Center Fitness Equipment Supply

**Issuing Organization:** Oakridge Community Recreation Centre
**RFP Reference:** OCRC26-07

## 1. Objective
Oakridge Community Recreation Centre seeks a vendor to supply, install, and maintain a full range of cardiovascular and strength training equipment for its newly expanded fitness facility, serving an estimated 3,000 active members.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of cardiovascular equipment (treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes)
- Supply and installation of strength training equipment (free weights, weight machines)
- Equipment layout planning in coordination with facility design
- Staff training on equipment operation and basic troubleshooting

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Equipment must be rated for commercial/high-frequency public use
- Vendor must provide minimum 5-year parts and labor warranty on cardiovascular equipment
- Equipment must include usage tracking/console technology compatible with member engagement goals
- Vendor must provide accessible equipment options suitable for users with mobility limitations

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized equipment pricing by category, along with an optional preventive maintenance contract priced separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Equipment supply and installation must be completed within 10 weeks of contract award, timed to align with the facility's planned reopening date.
