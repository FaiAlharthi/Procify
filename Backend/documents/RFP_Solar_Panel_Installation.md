# Example RFP: Municipal Solar Panel Installation

**Issuing Organization:** Town of Ashford
**RFP Reference:** TOA26-27

## 1. Objective
The Town of Ashford seeks a qualified solar energy contractor to design and install rooftop solar panel systems on four municipal buildings, including the town hall and public library, as part of its renewable energy initiative targeting a 30% reduction in municipal electricity costs.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Site assessment and system design for each of the four buildings
- Supply and installation of solar panel arrays and associated inverters
- Integration with existing electrical systems and the municipal utility grid
- Provision of a monitoring dashboard for ongoing energy production tracking

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Solar panels must carry a minimum 20-year performance warranty
- Installation must comply with current electrical code and utility interconnection requirements
- Contractor must provide projected annual energy output estimates per site
- System design must account for structural load capacity of each building's roof

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized pricing per site, along with available financing or lease-to-own options, and projected payback period based on estimated energy savings.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation across all four sites must be completed within 7 months of contract award, with sites completed sequentially to minimize disruption to building operations.
