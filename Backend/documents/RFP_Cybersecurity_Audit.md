# Example RFP: Cybersecurity Audit & Penetration Testing Services

**Issuing Organization:** Ferncroft Credit Union
**RFP Reference:** FCU26-15

## 1. Objective
Ferncroft Credit Union seeks a qualified cybersecurity firm to conduct an annual external penetration test and internal security audit of its core banking systems, network infrastructure, and customer-facing digital platforms, in support of regulatory compliance and risk management obligations.

## 2. Scope of Work
- External and internal penetration testing of all network segments
- Web and mobile application security testing for customer-facing platforms
- Social engineering assessment (phishing simulation) for staff
- Detailed findings report with severity ratings and remediation recommendations

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Testing team must hold relevant industry certifications (e.g., OSCP, CISSP)
- Firm must follow a recognized testing methodology (e.g., OWASP, NIST)
- All testing activities must be scheduled and scoped to avoid disruption to live banking operations
- Firm must sign a non-disclosure and data handling agreement prior to engagement

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed fee for the full annual testing scope, with optional pricing for quarterly re-testing of critical findings.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Testing must be completed within a 6-week window, with a draft report delivered within 2 weeks of testing completion and a final report within 4 weeks.
