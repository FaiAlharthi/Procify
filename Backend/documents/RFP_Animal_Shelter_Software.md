# Example RFP: Animal Shelter Management Software

**Issuing Organization:** Hollow Creek Humane Society
**RFP Reference:** HCHS26-05

## 1. Objective
Hollow Creek Humane Society seeks a vendor to supply a cloud-based animal shelter management platform to track intake, medical records, adoptions, and foster placements across its two facility locations, improving operational efficiency and public-facing adoption listings.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Animal intake and tracking system, including medical and behavioral records
- Public-facing adoptable animal listing integrated with the organization's website
- Foster and volunteer management module
- Donor and fundraising tracking integration

## 3. Technical Criteria
- System must support photo and document attachments per animal record
- Platform must be accessible via web browser and mobile devices for field staff
- Vendor must ensure data backup and recovery procedures are documented
- System must support data export for reporting to regulatory/licensing bodies

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include implementation costs separate from ongoing annual subscription fees, with pricing reflecting the organization's two-location, non-profit status.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Implementation and staff training must be completed within 3 months of contract award, with data migration from the current system completed prior to go-live.
