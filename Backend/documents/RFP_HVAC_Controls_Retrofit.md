# Example RFP: Building Automation and HVAC Controls Retrofit

**Issuing Organization:** Province of Eastmoor Facilities Management
**RFP Reference:** PEFM26-23

## 1. Objective
Eastmoor Facilities Management seeks a vendor to retrofit building automation and HVAC control systems across six government office buildings, improving energy efficiency and enabling centralized remote monitoring.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Installation of modern building automation system (BAS) controllers across all six sites
- Integration of HVAC, lighting, and access control systems under a centralized monitoring platform
- Decommissioning of legacy pneumatic/analog control systems
- Staff training for facilities management personnel on the new platform

## 3. Technical Criteria
- New system must support remote monitoring and control from a centralized dashboard
- Platform must provide energy usage analytics and reporting by building
- Vendor must ensure minimal disruption to building occupants during installation
- System must be compatible with the buildings' existing HVAC mechanical equipment where retained

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-building implementation pricing, with ongoing platform licensing and support costs itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation must be phased building-by-building, with full deployment across all six sites completed within 10 months of contract award.
