# Example RFP: Audio-Visual Equipment and Technical Support Services

**Issuing Organization:** Grandview Convention Centre
**RFP Reference:** GCC26-16

## 1. Objective
Grandview Convention Centre seeks a qualified vendor to provide audio-visual equipment rental and on-site technical support services for events held at the centre, supporting an estimated 200 events annually ranging from corporate meetings to large conferences.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply of audio, lighting, and video equipment for events of varying scale
- On-site technical staff for event setup, operation, and breakdown
- Live streaming and hybrid event technical support for select events
- Equipment maintenance and inventory management

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Vendor must maintain sufficient equipment inventory to support concurrent events at the centre
- Technical staff must demonstrate experience supporting comparable conference/event environments
- Vendor must provide backup equipment availability for critical event components
- Vendor must guarantee defined response times for on-site technical issues during live events

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include standard equipment package pricing by event size tier, with custom event quotes available for non-standard requirements.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Vendor must be ready to support events within 60 days of contract award, with an initial trial period covering a defined number of smaller events before full event calendar integration.
