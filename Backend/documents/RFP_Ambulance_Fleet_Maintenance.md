# Example RFP: Emergency Vehicle Fleet Maintenance Services

**Issuing Organization:** Northbridge Regional Emergency Medical Services
**RFP Reference:** NREMS26-11

## 1. Objective
Northbridge Regional EMS seeks a qualified service provider to deliver scheduled and emergency maintenance for its fleet of 18 ambulances and 6 support vehicles, ensuring maximum vehicle uptime and compliance with emergency vehicle safety standards.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Scheduled preventive maintenance for all fleet vehicles per manufacturer specifications
- 24/7 emergency repair response for in-service vehicle breakdowns
- Maintenance of emergency-specific equipment (lighting, sirens, power systems)
- Monthly fleet condition reporting to EMS fleet management

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Provider must offer guaranteed maximum response time for emergency repairs given the critical nature of the fleet
- Technicians must hold certification for emergency vehicle systems maintenance
- Provider must maintain adequate parts inventory to minimize vehicle downtime
- Provider must offer loaner or backup vehicle support during extended repairs

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include flat-rate pricing for scheduled maintenance packages and hourly rates for emergency repair work, including any after-hours premium rates.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service agreement to begin within 30 days of contract award, with an initial fleet condition assessment completed within the first 2 weeks of service.
