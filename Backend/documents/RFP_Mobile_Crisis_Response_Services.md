# Example RFP: Mobile Mental Health Crisis Response Services

**Issuing Organization:** Region of Dalewood Community Health Services
**RFP Reference:** RDCHS26-17

## 1. Objective
The Region of Dalewood seeks a qualified mental health services provider to operate a mobile crisis response team, providing community-based intervention as an alternative to traditional emergency response for non-violent mental health crisis calls.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Operation of a mobile crisis response team available during defined service hours
- Coordination with regional emergency dispatch services for appropriate call referral
- Follow-up support and referral to ongoing community mental health services
- Quarterly outcome reporting to regional health administration

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Crisis response staff must hold relevant mental health clinical qualifications
- Provider must demonstrate established protocols for safety assessment prior to dispatch
- Provider must maintain client confidentiality in compliance with applicable health privacy regulations
- Provider must demonstrate prior experience operating a comparable mobile crisis program

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed annual operating budget for the defined service hours and team composition, with options for service hour expansion priced separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Program must be operational within 5 months of contract award, including required staff hiring, training, and dispatch protocol coordination.
