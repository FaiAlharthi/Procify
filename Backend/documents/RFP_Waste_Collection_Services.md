# Example RFP: Residential Waste & Recycling Collection Services

**Issuing Organization:** Township of Elmsworth
**RFP Reference:** TOE26-08

## 1. Objective
The Township of Elmsworth seeks a qualified waste management company to provide weekly residential waste, recycling, and organics collection services for approximately 14,000 households over a 5-year contract term, with emphasis on increasing diversion rates from landfill.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Weekly curbside collection of household waste, recycling, and organics
- Supply of collection bins/carts to all households at contract start
- Seasonal bulk item and yard waste collection events
- Public education materials supporting waste diversion goals

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Collection vehicles must meet current emissions standards
- Contractor must provide collection route optimization to minimize missed collections
- Contractor must report monthly diversion rate data to the Township
- Contractor must maintain a customer service line for collection issues with defined response times

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-household annual pricing, with bin/cart supply costs itemized separately, and any proposed incentive structure tied to diversion rate improvements.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service must begin within 4 months of contract award, allowing time for bin distribution and public communication prior to collection start.
