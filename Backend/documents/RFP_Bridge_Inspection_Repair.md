# Example RFP: Bridge Inspection and Structural Repair Services

**Issuing Organization:** County of Ravenswood Public Works
**RFP Reference:** CRPW26-12

## 1. Objective
The County of Ravenswood seeks a qualified engineering firm to conduct structural inspections of 14 county-maintained bridges and perform identified priority repairs, ensuring continued safe operation and compliance with applicable bridge safety standards.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Full structural inspection of all 14 identified bridges
- Detailed condition reports with prioritized repair recommendations
- Execution of repair work for bridges identified as requiring immediate or high-priority attention
- Post-repair re-inspection and certification

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Inspection team must include licensed structural engineers with bridge inspection certification
- Firm must follow recognized bridge inspection standards and methodology
- Repair work must comply with current provincial/state bridge engineering standards
- Firm must provide traffic management plans for any bridges requiring lane closures during repair

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed fee for the full inspection scope, with repair work priced separately on a per-bridge basis following inspection findings.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
All inspections must be completed within 3 months of contract award, with priority repairs scheduled to begin within 6 months of inspection completion.
