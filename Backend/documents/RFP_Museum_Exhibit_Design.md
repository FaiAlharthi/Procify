# Example RFP: Museum Permanent Exhibit Design and Fabrication

**Issuing Organization:** Harborview Regional Museum
**RFP Reference:** HRM26-13

## 1. Objective
Harborview Regional Museum seeks a qualified exhibit design firm to design, fabricate, and install a new permanent exhibit on regional maritime history, occupying approximately 450 square meters of gallery space, with emphasis on interactive and accessible visitor experiences.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Exhibit concept design and storyline development in collaboration with museum curatorial staff
- Fabrication of physical exhibit elements, including interactive displays
- Integration of digital/multimedia components where appropriate
- Installation and final gallery setup

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Exhibit design must meet accessibility standards for visitor interaction and wayfinding
- All materials used must meet museum conservation standards for durability and safety
- Digital components must be designed for long-term maintainability by museum staff
- Firm must demonstrate prior experience with at least two comparable museum exhibit projects

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed fee broken down by design phase, fabrication, and installation, with any ongoing maintenance support priced separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Design development must be completed within 4 months of contract award, with fabrication and installation completed within an additional 6 months, targeting opening ahead of the museum's peak visitor season.
