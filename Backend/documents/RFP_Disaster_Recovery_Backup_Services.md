# Example RFP: IT Disaster Recovery and Backup Services

**Issuing Organization:** Province of Eastmoor Department of Finance
**RFP Reference:** PEDF26-14

## 1. Objective
The Department of Finance seeks a vendor to provide managed backup and disaster recovery services for its critical financial systems, ensuring defined recovery time and recovery point objectives in the event of a system outage or data loss incident.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Automated daily backup of all designated critical systems and databases
- Off-site/cloud-based disaster recovery environment capable of rapid failover
- Quarterly disaster recovery testing with documented results
- 24/7 monitoring and incident response support

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Vendor must guarantee a maximum recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) per system tier
- Backup data must be encrypted both in transit and at rest
- Disaster recovery environment must be geographically separated from primary production systems
- Vendor must hold relevant data security certifications appropriate to government data handling

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed annual fee covering the full backup and disaster recovery scope, with any additional costs for expanded system coverage itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Initial backup and disaster recovery environment setup must be completed within 3 months of contract award, with the first full disaster recovery test conducted within 4 months.
