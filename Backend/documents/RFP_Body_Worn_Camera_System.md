# Example RFP: Body-Worn Camera System for Police Services

**Issuing Organization:** Kingsford Police Service
**RFP Reference:** KPS26-08

## 1. Objective
Kingsford Police Service seeks a vendor to supply, implement, and maintain a body-worn camera system for approximately 180 frontline officers, supporting transparency, evidence collection, and public accountability objectives.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply of body-worn camera hardware for all frontline officers
- Secure cloud-based video storage and evidence management platform
- Integration with existing case management and evidence handling systems
- Officer training on equipment use and evidence handling protocols

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Video storage platform must maintain chain-of-custody integrity for evidentiary use
- System must support configurable retention periods per video category in compliance with applicable evidence retention regulations
- Cameras must provide minimum battery life sufficient for a full standard patrol shift
- Vendor must ensure data storage complies with applicable law enforcement data security standards

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hardware costs separate from ongoing per-officer monthly storage and platform licensing fees.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Phased rollout must begin within 4 months of contract award, with full department-wide deployment completed within 8 months.
