# Example RFP: MRI Equipment Supply and Installation

**Issuing Organization:** Cedarbrook Regional Hospital
**RFP Reference:** CRH26-14

## 1. Objective
Cedarbrook Regional Hospital seeks a qualified vendor to supply, install, and maintain a new 3T MRI system to replace its current aging unit, improving diagnostic imaging capacity and reducing patient wait times for imaging services.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of a 3T MRI system, including required shielding and room modifications
- Removal and disposal of the existing MRI unit
- Staff training for radiology technologists and supporting clinical staff
- Integration with the hospital's existing PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System)

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Equipment must hold current applicable medical device regulatory approval
- Vendor must provide minimum 24/7 service support with guaranteed response time given critical care implications
- System must be compatible with existing PACS and electronic health record systems
- Vendor must provide uptime guarantee with defined penalty/credit terms for extended outages

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include equipment, installation, and room modification costs, along with a proposed multi-year service and maintenance contract.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation must be scheduled to minimize disruption to ongoing imaging services, with a target of no more than 3 weeks of full imaging suite downtime during transition.
