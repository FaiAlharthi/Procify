# Example RFP: Municipal Composting Facility Operations

**Issuing Organization:** Regional District of Fairhaven
**RFP Reference:** RDF26-21

## 1. Objective
The Regional District of Fairhaven seeks a qualified operator to manage day-to-day operations of its municipal composting facility, processing approximately 25,000 tonnes of organic waste annually from residential collection programs across the region.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Daily receiving, processing, and turning of incoming organic waste material
- Quality control monitoring throughout the composting process
- Finished compost product testing and distribution coordination
- Odor and environmental monitoring per facility permit requirements

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Operator must demonstrate prior experience operating a comparable-scale composting facility
- Facility operations must comply with all applicable environmental permit conditions
- Operator must maintain documented contamination screening procedures for incoming material
- Operator must provide regular water and air quality monitoring reports to the Regional District

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a per-tonne processing fee structure, with any revenue-sharing terms for finished compost product sales described if applicable.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Operations transition must be completed within 4 months of contract award, including a transition period with the current operator to ensure continuity of service.
