# Example RFP: Tourism Marketing Agency Services

**Issuing Organization:** Lakeview County Tourism Board
**RFP Reference:** LCTB26-04

## 1. Objective
Lakeview County Tourism Board seeks a marketing agency to develop and execute a 2-year tourism promotion campaign aimed at increasing regional visitor numbers by 15%, with focus on digital marketing, content creation, and partnership development with regional tourism operators.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Development of a comprehensive marketing strategy targeting key visitor demographics
- Production of digital content, including photography, video, and social media assets
- Management of paid digital advertising campaigns across relevant platforms
- Coordination with local tourism operators for co-marketing initiatives

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Agency must demonstrate prior experience in destination/tourism marketing with at least two reference campaigns
- Agency must provide a content approval workflow allowing Board review prior to publication
- All campaign analytics and reporting must be provided through a shared, accessible dashboard
- Agency must comply with applicable advertising standards and disclosure regulations

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a monthly retainer fee structure, with media/advertising spend budgeted and itemized separately from agency service fees.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Campaign strategy and initial content must be ready for launch within 10 weeks of contract award, with quarterly performance reviews throughout the 2-year engagement.
