# Example RFP: Aquatic Centre Lifeguard and Pool Operations Staffing

**Issuing Organization:** Township of Birchwood Recreation Services
**RFP Reference:** TBRS26-12

## 1. Objective
The Township of Birchwood seeks a qualified staffing agency to provide certified lifeguard and pool operations staff for its two public aquatic centres, ensuring full operational coverage during public swim hours and scheduled programming.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Provision of certified lifeguards for all public swim sessions and scheduled aquatic programs
- Provision of certified pool operators for water quality and chemical management
- Staff scheduling coordination with Township recreation programming calendar
- Incident reporting and documentation per Township safety protocols

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All lifeguard staff must hold current, valid lifeguarding and first aid/CPR certification
- Pool operations staff must hold relevant pool operator certification
- Agency must maintain adequate staffing reserves to cover unplanned absences without service disruption
- Agency must conduct background checks for all staff placed in roles involving direct contact with children

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hourly billing rates by staff role, with any premium rates for holiday or extended-hours coverage itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Staffing must be fully in place within 60 days of contract award, ahead of the start of the summer aquatic programming season.
