# Example RFP: Data Center Colocation Services

**Issuing Organization:** Province of Easterholt Digital Services Branch
**RFP Reference:** PEDS26-18

## 1. Objective
The Digital Services Branch seeks a qualified data center provider to deliver colocation services for government servers and storage infrastructure, ensuring high availability, physical security, and disaster recovery readiness for critical public-facing systems.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Provision of rack space, power, and cooling for government server infrastructure
- 24/7 physical security and access control at the facility
- Network connectivity with guaranteed bandwidth allocation
- On-site hands-on remote support for hardware issues outside business hours

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Facility must maintain a minimum Tier III data center classification
- Power infrastructure must include redundant backup generation with documented failover testing
- Facility must be located within the province to comply with data residency requirements
- Provider must maintain current recognized data center security certifications

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include monthly recurring costs for rack space, power, and bandwidth, with any setup/onboarding fees itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Onboarding and migration of existing infrastructure must be completed within 4 months of contract award, with a documented migration plan minimizing service downtime.
