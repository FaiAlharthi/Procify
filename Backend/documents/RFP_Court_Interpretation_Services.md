# Example RFP: Court Interpretation Services

**Issuing Organization:** Bramwell Judicial District
**RFP Reference:** BJD26-29

## 1. Objective
The Bramwell Judicial District seeks a qualified language services provider to deliver in-person and remote interpretation services across multiple languages for court proceedings, ensuring equitable access to justice for non-English-speaking participants.

## 2. Scope of Work
- On-demand in-person interpretation for scheduled court proceedings
- Remote video/phone interpretation for time-sensitive or lower-priority matters
- Coverage across a defined list of commonly required languages, with on-call access to less common languages
- Scheduling coordination system integrated with the court's case management calendar

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Interpreters must hold recognized court interpretation certification for their respective languages
- Provider must guarantee minimum coverage availability for high-demand languages
- Remote interpretation technology must meet courtroom audio/video quality standards
- Provider must maintain interpreter conflict-of-interest screening procedures

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hourly rates by language and service type (in-person vs. remote), with any minimum booking fees or cancellation policies clearly stated.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service must be operational within 60 days of contract award, with a 30-day trial period during which service quality will be jointly reviewed.
