# Example RFP: Municipal Road Snow Removal Services

**Issuing Organization:** Township of Birchwood
**RFP Reference:** TOB26-02

## 1. Objective
The Township of Birchwood seeks a contractor to provide snow plowing and de-icing services for approximately 85 km of municipal roads over a 3-year winter season contract, ensuring road safety and timely clearance during winter weather events.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Snow plowing of all designated municipal roads per the Township's priority route map
- Application of de-icing materials on priority routes
- Sidewalk clearance adjacent to municipal buildings
- 24-hour on-call availability during active winter weather events

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Contractor must maintain a minimum fleet of equipment sufficient to clear all priority routes within defined time targets after snowfall ends
- Equipment must include GPS tracking accessible to the Township for route verification
- Contractor must provide backup equipment/operator plans in case of mechanical failure during an active event
- Contractor must carry adequate liability insurance for road maintenance operations

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a seasonal flat-rate price covering all routes, with a defined per-event rate structure for any extreme weather events exceeding normal seasonal scope.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Services must be ready to commence by November 1 each contract year, with equipment readiness verification completed by October 15.
