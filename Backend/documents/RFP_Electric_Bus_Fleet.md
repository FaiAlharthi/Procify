# Example RFP: Electric Transit Bus Fleet Acquisition

**Issuing Organization:** Greater Oakridge Transit Commission
**RFP Reference:** GOTC26-33

## 1. Objective
The Greater Oakridge Transit Commission seeks a qualified manufacturer to supply 25 battery-electric transit buses to replace aging diesel buses, supporting the Commission's goal of a fully electric fleet by 2032, along with associated charging infrastructure.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply of 25 battery-electric low-floor transit buses meeting accessibility requirements
- Supply and installation of depot charging infrastructure sized for the new fleet
- Operator and maintenance staff training on electric bus systems
- Spare parts package and technical documentation

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Buses must achieve a minimum operational range suitable for the Commission's longest daily route without mid-day charging
- Buses must comply with current accessibility standards for low-floor boarding and securement areas
- Charging infrastructure must be compatible with the depot's existing electrical capacity, or vendor must specify required upgrades
- Vendor must disclose battery warranty terms and expected degradation over the warranty period

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized per-bus pricing, charging infrastructure costs, and a proposed multi-year parts and service agreement.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Buses must be delivered in two phases over 18 months, with charging infrastructure installation completed prior to first bus delivery.
