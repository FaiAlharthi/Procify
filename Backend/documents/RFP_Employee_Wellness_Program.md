# Example RFP: Employee Wellness Program Services

**Issuing Organization:** Briarcliff Manufacturing Corporation
**RFP Reference:** BMC26-06

## 1. Objective
Briarcliff Manufacturing Corporation seeks a qualified wellness program provider to design and deliver an employee wellness program for its 1,100 employees across two manufacturing sites, aimed at reducing health-related absenteeism and improving overall employee wellbeing.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Annual health risk assessments and biometric screening events at both sites
- On-demand access to mental health and counseling support services
- Wellness education programming (nutrition, stress management, ergonomics)
- Quarterly engagement reporting to HR leadership

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Provider must ensure all health data collection complies with applicable privacy and employment health regulations
- Provider must offer multilingual program materials given the company's diverse workforce
- Counseling services must be available via phone, video, and in-person options
- Provider must demonstrate experience delivering programs to comparable manufacturing workforce sizes

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a per-employee annual program fee, with any additional costs for on-site screening events itemized separately.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Program design and provider onboarding must be completed within 8 weeks of contract award, with the first biometric screening event scheduled within 4 months.
