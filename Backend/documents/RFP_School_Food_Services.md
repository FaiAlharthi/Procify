# Example RFP: School District Food Services Provider

**Issuing Organization:** Pinegrove School District
**RFP Reference:** PSD26-09

## 1. Objective
Pinegrove School District seeks a qualified food services provider to manage daily meal preparation and service across 12 elementary and secondary schools, serving approximately 6,500 students, with emphasis on nutritional standards and food allergy management.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Daily breakfast and lunch meal preparation and service at all 12 school sites
- Menu planning compliant with national school nutrition guidelines
- Dedicated allergen-free meal preparation line for students with documented food allergies
- Monthly nutritional reporting to district administration

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Provider must hold current food safety certification for all kitchen staff
- Provider must maintain documented allergen control procedures, including cross-contamination prevention
- All food preparation facilities must pass district health and safety inspection prior to contract start
- Provider must disclose any food safety violations within the past 5 years

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-meal pricing broken down by breakfast and lunch, along with any additional fees for special dietary accommodations.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Service must begin at the start of the upcoming school year, with a 2-week trial period at two pilot schools prior to district-wide rollout.
