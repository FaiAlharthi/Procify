# Example RFP: Fleet Telematics and GPS Tracking System

**Issuing Organization:** Province of Eastmoor Highway Maintenance Division
**RFP Reference:** PEHM26-24

## 1. Objective
The Eastmoor Highway Maintenance Division seeks a vendor to supply a fleet telematics and GPS tracking system for its 220-vehicle maintenance and snow removal fleet, improving route efficiency, fuel management, and driver safety monitoring.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of GPS tracking hardware across the full fleet
- Real-time fleet location and route tracking dashboard
- Driver behavior monitoring (speeding, harsh braking, idling)
- Fuel usage and maintenance scheduling integration

## 3. Technical Criteria
- System must provide real-time location updates at a minimum defined refresh interval
- Platform must be accessible to dispatch staff via web and mobile interfaces
- Vendor must ensure driver data is handled in compliance with applicable employee privacy regulations
- Hardware must be rated for reliable operation in extreme winter operating conditions

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hardware and installation costs separate from ongoing per-vehicle monthly subscription fees.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation across the full fleet must be completed within 4 months of contract award, ahead of the upcoming winter maintenance season.
