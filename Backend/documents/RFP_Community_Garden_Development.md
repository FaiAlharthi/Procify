# Example RFP: Community Garden Development Services

**Issuing Organization:** City of Sutterfield Parks & Environment Office
**RFP Reference:** CSPE26-03

## 1. Objective
The City of Sutterfield seeks a qualified landscape contractor to design and construct a new community garden facility on a 0.8-hectare municipal lot, providing approximately 120 individual garden plots and shared amenity space for residents.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Site grading and soil preparation for garden plots
- Construction of raised garden beds, including accessible plot options
- Installation of a shared irrigation system
- Construction of a small shared tool shed and seating area

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Soil used for garden beds must meet applicable standards for food-growing safety
- At least 10% of plots must be designed for accessible/wheelchair use
- Irrigation system must include water-metering for usage tracking
- Contractor must demonstrate prior experience with at least one comparable community garden project

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed-price bid for the full scope of work, with optional pricing for an expanded composting area if desired by the City.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Construction must be completed within 4 months of contract award, targeting completion ahead of the regional spring planting season.
