# Example RFP: Outside Legal Counsel Services

**Issuing Organization:** City of Marlowe
**RFP Reference:** COM26-22

## 1. Objective
The City of Marlowe seeks to retain a qualified law firm to provide outside legal counsel services on an as-needed basis, supplementing the City's internal legal department for matters including labor relations, land use, and municipal litigation, over a 3-year term.

## 2. Scope of Work
- General municipal legal advisory services on an as-needed basis
- Representation in labor relations and collective bargaining matters
- Land use and zoning-related legal support
- Litigation support and representation as required

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Firm must demonstrate minimum 10 years of municipal law experience
- Firm must disclose any conflicts of interest with current City contracts or ongoing matters
- Assigned lead counsel must hold current good standing with the relevant bar association
- Firm must provide references from at least two other municipal clients

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hourly rates by attorney seniority level, along with any proposed retainer or capped-fee arrangements for predictable recurring matters.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Engagement to begin within 30 days of contract award, with a quarterly review of engagement scope and billing with the City Attorney's office.
