# Example RFP: Aerial Drone Survey Services

**Issuing Organization:** Coastal Ridge Conservation Authority
**RFP Reference:** CRCA26-18

## 1. Objective
Coastal Ridge Conservation Authority seeks a qualified drone services provider to conduct periodic aerial surveys of protected wetland areas, supporting environmental monitoring and erosion tracking across a 1,200-hectare conservation zone.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Quarterly aerial drone surveys of designated conservation zones
- High-resolution imagery and topographic mapping deliverables
- Year-over-year erosion and vegetation change analysis
- Data delivery in formats compatible with the Authority's GIS systems

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Drone operators must hold current applicable aviation authority certification for commercial drone operation
- Imagery resolution must meet a minimum defined ground sampling distance for accurate change detection
- Provider must carry liability insurance covering drone operations
- Data must be delivered within agreed formats and coordinate reference systems

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-survey pricing, with annual contract pricing for the full quarterly survey schedule, and any additional fees for rush deliverables.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
First survey must be completed within 6 weeks of contract award, with subsequent surveys delivered quarterly per the agreed schedule over a 2-year term.
