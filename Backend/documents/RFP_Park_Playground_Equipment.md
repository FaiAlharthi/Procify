# Example RFP: Public Park Playground Equipment Replacement

**Issuing Organization:** City of Brookhaven Parks Department
**RFP Reference:** CBPD26-19

## 1. Objective
The City of Brookhaven Parks Department seeks a vendor to supply and install new playground equipment at five neighborhood parks, replacing equipment that no longer meets current safety standards, with priority given to inclusive/accessible play features.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Removal and disposal of existing playground equipment at five identified park sites
- Supply and installation of new playground structures, including accessible/inclusive play components
- Installation of compliant safety surfacing at each site
- Site restoration following installation (landscaping, fencing as needed)

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All equipment must meet current national playground safety standards
- At least one play structure per site must be wheelchair-accessible
- Safety surfacing must meet required impact attenuation standards for fall height
- Vendor must provide a minimum 10-year structural warranty on equipment

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include itemized pricing per site, covering equipment, installation, surfacing, and site restoration as separate line items.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation across all five sites must be completed within 5 months of contract award, scheduled to avoid the peak summer park usage period where possible.
