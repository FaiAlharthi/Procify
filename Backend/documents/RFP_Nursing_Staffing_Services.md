# Example RFP: Supplemental Nursing Staffing Services

**Issuing Organization:** Willowmere Long-Term Care Facility
**RFP Reference:** WLTC26-10

## 1. Objective
Willowmere Long-Term Care Facility seeks a qualified healthcare staffing agency to provide supplemental registered nurse and personal support worker staffing to cover shift gaps and seasonal demand fluctuations across its 180-bed facility.

## 2. Scope of Work
- On-demand placement of registered nurses and personal support workers for shift coverage
- Pre-screened, credential-verified staff pool available on short notice
- Orientation support for placed staff on facility-specific procedures
- Monthly utilization and performance reporting

## 3. Technical Criteria
- All placed staff must hold current, verified professional licensing/certification
- Agency must conduct criminal background checks and reference verification prior to placement
- Agency must guarantee a minimum fill rate for shift requests submitted within a defined notice period
- Agency must maintain liability insurance covering placed staff

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hourly billing rates by staff category, including any premium rates for short-notice or overnight shift placements.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Agency must be ready to begin placements within 30 days of contract award, with an initial 90-day performance review period.
