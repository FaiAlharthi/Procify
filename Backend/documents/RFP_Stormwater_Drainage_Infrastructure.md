# Example RFP: Stormwater Drainage Infrastructure Upgrade

**Issuing Organization:** Village of Thistledown
**RFP Reference:** VOT26-20

## 1. Objective
The Village of Thistledown seeks a qualified contractor to upgrade aging stormwater drainage infrastructure in its low-lying downtown district, addressing recurring flooding issues during heavy rainfall events.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Replacement of undersized stormwater piping along three identified downtown streets
- Installation of additional catch basins at flood-prone intersections
- Construction of a stormwater retention area on village-owned land adjacent to the district
- Restoration of road surfaces and landscaping following construction

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Design must be sized to handle a defined storm event return period per current municipal engineering standards
- Contractor must coordinate with the village to minimize disruption to downtown business access during construction
- All work must comply with applicable environmental regulations for stormwater management
- Contractor must provide a minimum 5-year warranty on installed infrastructure

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed-price bid for the full scope of work, with a contingency allowance recommendation and milestone-based payment schedule.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Construction must be completed within 8 months of contract award, with work sequenced to avoid the village's peak summer tourism season where possible.
