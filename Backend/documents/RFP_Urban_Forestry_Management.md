# Example RFP: Urban Forestry Management and Tree Planting Services

**Issuing Organization:** City of Ellsworth Parks & Urban Forestry Division
**RFP Reference:** CEPF26-28

## 1. Objective
The City of Ellsworth seeks a qualified arboriculture contractor to provide urban forestry management services, including tree planting, pruning, and removal across municipal boulevards and parks, supporting the City's goal of increasing urban tree canopy coverage by 8% over five years.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Planting of approximately 2,000 trees annually across designated boulevard and park locations
- Scheduled pruning and maintenance of existing municipal tree inventory
- Removal of hazardous or diseased trees identified through inspection
- Annual tree inventory update and condition reporting

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Contractor must employ certified arborists for inspection, pruning, and removal work
- Planted tree species must align with the City's approved urban forestry species list
- Contractor must follow industry-standard pruning practices to maintain tree health
- Contractor must carry adequate liability insurance for work near public roadways and utilities

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include per-unit pricing for planting, pruning, and removal services, with annual contract pricing based on projected volume by service category.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Services must begin within 60 days of contract award, with annual planting work scheduled during the appropriate regional planting season each contract year.
