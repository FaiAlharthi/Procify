# Example RFP: New Student Residence Hall Construction

**Issuing Organization:** Thornfield University
**RFP Reference:** TU26-40

## 1. Objective
Thornfield University seeks a qualified general contractor to design and construct a new 400-bed student residence hall to address growing on-campus housing demand, targeting LEED Silver certification or equivalent sustainability standard.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Full design-build services for a 400-bed residence hall, including common areas, study spaces, and a ground-floor dining facility
- Site preparation and utility connections
- Furnishing of all student rooms per university specifications
- Landscaping and exterior site work

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Design must achieve LEED Silver certification or equivalent recognized sustainability standard
- Building must comply with current accessibility and fire/life safety codes
- Contractor must demonstrate prior experience with at least two comparable student housing projects
- Contractor must provide a structural warranty of minimum 10 years

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a guaranteed maximum price (GMP) for the full design-build scope, with a detailed cost breakdown by major building system.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Construction must be completed in time for student move-in at the start of the fall academic term, approximately 22 months from contract award.
