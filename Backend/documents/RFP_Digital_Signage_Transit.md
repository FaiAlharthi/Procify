# Example RFP: Digital Passenger Information Signage System

**Issuing Organization:** Riverside Metro Transit
**RFP Reference:** RMT26-27

## 1. Objective
Riverside Metro Transit seeks a vendor to supply and install digital passenger information signage across 30 transit stations, providing real-time arrival information and service alerts to improve the rider experience.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of digital display screens at all 30 station platforms
- Integration with the transit authority's existing real-time vehicle tracking system
- Content management system for service alerts and advertising content
- Remote monitoring and diagnostics for all installed displays

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Displays must be rated for outdoor/semi-outdoor operation where applicable, with adequate sunlight readability
- System must support real-time data refresh at a minimum defined interval
- Content management platform must support role-based access for authorized staff
- Vendor must provide a guaranteed uptime commitment with defined remediation timelines for display outages

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include hardware and installation costs, separate from ongoing software licensing and maintenance support fees.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Installation must be completed in phases across station groups, with full deployment finished within 7 months of contract award.
