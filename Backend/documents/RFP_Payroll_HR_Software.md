# Example RFP: Payroll & HR Management Software

**Issuing Organization:** Dunmore County Administration
**RFP Reference:** DCA26-31

## 1. Objective
Dunmore County Administration seeks a vendor to supply a cloud-based payroll and human resources management system to replace its current on-premises software, supporting approximately 850 employees across multiple county departments with varying pay schedules and union agreements.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Payroll processing supporting multiple pay schedules and union/non-union employee categories
- Employee self-service portal for pay stubs, tax documents, and benefits enrollment
- Time and attendance tracking integration
- Standard and custom reporting for finance and HR departments

## 3. Technical Criteria
- System must support compliance with applicable provincial/federal payroll tax requirements
- Platform must provide audit trails for all payroll adjustments
- Vendor must support data migration from the county's existing payroll database
- System must include role-based access controls for sensitive employee data

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include implementation costs separate from ongoing per-employee monthly licensing fees, with any data migration costs itemized.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Implementation, testing, and parallel-run with the legacy system must be completed within 6 months of contract award, with full cutover targeted for the start of a new fiscal year.
