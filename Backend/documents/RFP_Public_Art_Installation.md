# Example RFP: Public Art Installation - Riverside Plaza

**Issuing Organization:** City of Falston Arts & Culture Office
**RFP Reference:** CFAC26-15

## 1. Objective
The City of Falston seeks a qualified artist or artist team to design, fabricate, and install a permanent public art piece for the newly developed Riverside Plaza, reflecting the city's cultural heritage and waterfront identity.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Concept design development in consultation with the City's public art selection committee
- Fabrication of the approved art installation
- Site preparation coordination and installation
- Provision of a maintenance plan for the artwork's expected lifespan

## 3. Technical Criteria
- Artwork must be designed for outdoor durability appropriate to the regional climate
- Materials and structural design must meet public safety standards for a publicly accessible space
- Artist must provide a maintenance and cleaning guide for City parks staff
- Artist must demonstrate prior experience with at least one comparable public art commission

## 4. Commercial Template
Proposals must include a fixed total commission fee covering design, fabrication, and installation, with any ongoing maintenance support priced separately if offered.

## 5. Project Delivery & Deadlines
Design concept must be finalized within 3 months of contract award, with fabrication and installation completed within an additional 6 months.
